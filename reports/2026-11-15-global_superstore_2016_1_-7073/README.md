# Global Superstore 2016 Sales Analysis

This analysis explores a dataset of global retail operations from 2016, covering orders, returns, and regional personnel. The dataset includes over 51,000 transactions across various markets and product categories.

### Key Findings
- **Technology** is the highest-performing category, generating over **$4.7 million** in total sales.
- The **Europe** market stands out as the most profitable region, contributing approximately **$451,745** to the total profit.
- Over **51,200 orders** were processed with an average shipping cost of **$26.48** per transaction.
- Total global profit for the period reached **$1.47 million** on sales of **$12.65 million**.

### Dashboard Features
- **Bar Chart**: Displays sales by sub-category. You can use the **Market** filter to see performance in specific regions like Africa, Asia Pacific, or Europe.
- **Pie Chart**: Shows the distribution of sales across customer segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office). Use the **Category** filter to narrow down the view to Technology, Furniture, or Office Supplies.

The logic used to process this data and generate the dashboard is contained in the `analysis.py` file.
