# Global Superstore Sales Analysis

This analysis examines global sales performance, profitability, and customer segmentation for the Global Superstore dataset. It provides a comprehensive view of how different product categories and market segments contribute to the overall business.

### Key Findings
- **Total Revenue**: The store generated a total of **$12,649,677** in sales.
- **Top Category**: **Technology** is the highest revenue-generating category, accounting for **$4,748,657**.
- **Market Profitability**: **Europe** stands out as the most profitable market, with a total profit of **$451,745**.
- **Efficiency**: Shipping costs represent **10.74%** of total revenue, while the average discount offered is **14.29%**.
- **Order Volume**: A total of **25,728** unique orders were processed during the period.

### Interactive Charts
- **Sales by Sub-Category (Bar Chart)**: Shows the top 15 sub-categories by sales revenue. You can use the **Category filter** to drill down into specific product groups (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology).
- **Sales by Customer Segment (Pie Chart)**: Illustrates the distribution of sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments. Use the **Market filter** to see how these segments perform in different global regions.

The analysis was performed using a custom Python script (`analysis.py`) that processed the raw data and generated the interactive dashboard (`report.html`).
