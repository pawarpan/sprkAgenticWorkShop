# Global Superstore 2016 Sales Analysis

This dataset contains detailed sales order records from a global superstore for the year 2016, including information on order dates, shipping, customer segments, product categories, sales, profits, discounts, and regions. It covers over 51,000 orders with rich categorical and numeric data, enabling comprehensive sales and profit analysis across different dimensions such as product category, customer segment, and geographic region.

## Key Insights
- Total sales amounted to approximately $12.6 million.
- Total profit across all orders was about $1.47 million.
- There were 17,415 unique customers and 25,728 unique orders.
- The average discount given was around 14.3%.
- The maximum single order profit was $8,400, while the maximum loss on a single order was nearly -$6,600.
- The largest single order sales value was $22,638.
- The highest quantity ordered in a single order was 14 units.
- The maximum discount given on any order was 85%.
