# Global Superstore 2016 Sales Analysis

This dataset contains detailed transactional data for a global superstore's orders in 2016, including sales, profit, shipping, and product details across multiple regions, markets, and customer segments. It covers over 51,000 orders with rich categorical and numeric attributes such as Category, Segment, Region, Sales, Profit, and Order Priority.

## Key Insights
- Total sales reached $12,641,000 across 3 product categories.
- Profit summed to $1,472,000 with an average discount of 14.29%.
- The highest sales month was 2014-11.
- The most profitable category was Technology.
- Orders were mostly shipped by Standard Class with 22,000+ shipments.
