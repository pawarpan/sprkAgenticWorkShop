# Global Superstore 2016 Sales Analysis

This dataset contains detailed sales order records from a global superstore in 2016, including 51,292 orders with attributes such as order dates, shipping modes, customer segments, product categories, regions, sales, profit, quantity, discounts, and taxes. The data spans multiple regions and markets worldwide, providing a comprehensive view of sales performance and profitability.

## Key Insights
- Total sales reached $12,645,000 across 1430 unique order dates (months and days).
- Total profit was $1,471,000, with an average profit per order of $28.73.
- The dataset includes 17,415 unique customers.
- Average discount given was 14.29%.
- Orders were shipped using 4 different ship modes and belong to 3 customer segments.
