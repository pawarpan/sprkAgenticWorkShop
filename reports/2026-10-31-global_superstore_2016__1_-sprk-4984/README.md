- The average discount given across all orders is 14.3%.

- The total shipping cost incurred is around $1.36 million.
- The most profitable product category is Technology, generating over $1.1 million in profit.
- The region with the highest sales is Central US, with sales exceeding $3.1 million.
- The segment with the highest average discount is Consumer, averaging 17.4% discount.
- The month with the highest sales is 2014-11, with sales totaling approximately $1.2 million.
- The most frequent shipping mode is Standard Class, used in over 28,000 orders.
